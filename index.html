What does RACI mean?

R

Responsible

The person (or people) who do the work. Every task must have at least one R. Multiple Rs mean shared execution — but too many can cause confusion about who leads.

A

Accountable

The single person who is ultimately answerable for the outcome. They approve the work and are on the hook if it doesn't happen. There must be exactly one A per activity.

C

Consulted

People whose input is sought before decisions are made. This is a two-way conversation — their feedback must be considered. Overusing C slows things down; be selective.

I

Informed

People who are kept up to date on progress or decisions, but not involved in making them. One-way communication only. Use I when someone needs visibility but not a voice.
